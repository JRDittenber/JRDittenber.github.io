Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy is based on high expectations, high support, and disciplined practice. I believe students learn quantitative material best when difficult ideas are broken into clear steps, connected to meaningful examples, and reinforced through active problem solving.

I aim to help students not only complete assignments, but also develop mathematical maturity, statistical reasoning, technical confidence, and stronger work habits. In mathematics, statistics, data science, and machine learning, my goal is to help students see both the structure of the ideas and their practical usefulness.
